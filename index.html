Education and local law

Aletheia does not replace or override the educational laws in force in any country. Where conventional schooling is required, it serves as a complement for families. Where home education is permitted, it can provide comprehensive organization and support. Every family remains responsible for observing its local requirements.

Questions and answers

What families should know.

Is Aletheia a school?

No. Aletheia is an educational organization and support platform for families, educators, and communities.

Does it replace the curriculum chosen by the family?

No. Families can organize and adapt curricula, methods, materials, and routines according to their goals and local requirements.

Can different educational methods be used?

Yes. Aletheia is designed to support different methods and rhythms without imposing a single educational model.

How will artificial intelligence be used?

As an assistant for planning, reflection, review, and organization, always under human and parental supervision.

Does Aletheia replace legal schooling requirements?

No. The platform follows the educational context of each country: it complements conventional schooling where attendance is mandatory and provides comprehensive organization and support where home education is legally permitted. Families must always comply with applicable local law.
